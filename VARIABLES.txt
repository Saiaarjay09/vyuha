  VYUHA - EVERY VARIABLE, AND HOW EACH ONE MOVES MARKETS
================================================================================

  46 catalogued sources, 108 declared series, 13 domains.
  Lines marked [LIVE] have a fetcher verified against the live source.
  Lines marked [PLANNED] are catalogued but not yet wired.
  Lines marked [BLOCKED] were usable and are not any more.

  Generated against the catalogue in src/vyuha/ingest/catalogue.py. Run
  `vyuha sources list` for the current machine-readable state.

  A NOTE ON "CAUSES". Everything below describes a transmission mechanism -
  a plausible, usually well-documented path from variable to price. A
  mechanism is not proof that the variable predicts returns today. Vyuha
  tests candidate variables out of sample with false-discovery-rate control
  before trusting any of them (docs/LEARNING.md). Read these as hypotheses
  with good priors, not as established effects.


================================================================================
  1. POLICY RATES AND MONEY  [LIVE]
================================================================================

REPO_RATE - the RBI's policy rate
  The single most important number in Indian finance. It sets the price of
  overnight money, which propagates to everything:
    -> bank lending rates move within weeks (most Indian loans are now
       externally benchmarked to the repo, so transmission is fast)
    -> the discount rate applied to future company earnings changes, so
       equity valuations move inversely: higher repo, lower P/E multiples
    -> the gap between Indian and US rates changes, which moves the rupee
       and therefore foreign investor appetite
    -> high-debt companies are hit hardest; a leveraged infrastructure firm
       reprices far more than a cash-rich IT exporter
  Direction: repo UP -> equities DOWN (especially rate-sensitive sectors:
  banks, real estate, autos), bond prices DOWN, rupee UP.

SDF_RATE / MSF_RATE - the floor and ceiling of the policy corridor
  Together with the repo they define how far overnight rates can drift. A
  narrowing corridor signals the RBI wants tighter control of liquidity.

CRR - cash reserve ratio
  The share of deposits banks must park with the RBI, earning nothing. A CRR
  rise removes liquidity from the banking system directly and immediately -
  faster-acting than a repo change, and it hits bank profitability, because
  the impounded cash earns zero.
  Direction: CRR UP -> bank stocks DOWN, short-term rates UP.

SLR - statutory liquidity ratio
  The share of deposits banks must hold in government securities. It creates
  captive demand for G-secs, which is why Indian government borrowing costs
  are lower than the fiscal position alone would imply. Changes alter that
  captive demand and move the whole yield curve.

BANK_RATE / REVERSE_REPO_RATE
  Largely signalling instruments now, but a change is read as intent.


================================================================================
  2. INFLATION  [CPI: PLANNED via MOSPI, PARTIAL via FRED/OECD]
================================================================================

CPI_COMBINED_YOY - headline consumer inflation
  The RBI targets 4% with a 2-6% tolerance band. Breaching 6% for three
  consecutive quarters legally obliges the RBI to explain itself to
  Parliament, which makes the band a hard behavioural constraint, not a
  preference.
  Chain: CPI UP -> RBI holds or hikes -> discount rate UP -> equity
  multiples DOWN and bond prices DOWN.
  PUBLICATION LAG ~12 DAYS and revised twice. This is the single clearest
  reason Vyuha's store is bitemporal: on 5 April nobody knew March CPI.

CPI_FOOD_YOY - food inflation
  Food is ~46% of India's CPI basket, roughly double the weight in a
  developed economy. This is why Indian monetary policy is hostage to the
  monsoon in a way the Fed's is not. A bad harvest can force tightening into
  an economic slowdown - the worst combination for equities.

CPI_CORE_YOY - core inflation
  Strips food and fuel. If core is contained while headline spikes, the RBI
  can credibly call the spike transitory and hold. If core is rising too,
  it cannot.

WPI_YOY - wholesale prices
  Leads CPI for manufactured goods and is a direct read on corporate input
  costs. A widening WPI-CPI gap means companies are absorbing costs they
  cannot pass on - margin compression is coming.


================================================================================
  3. GROWTH AND REAL ACTIVITY
================================================================================

IIP_YOY - industrial production  [PLANNED; PARTIAL via FRED/OECD]
  ~42 DAY LAG and heavily revised, which makes it nearly useless for timing.
  Vyuha's intended answer is to nowcast it from power demand and e-way bills,
  both of which arrive weeks earlier.

GDP_YOY  [PLANNED]
  ~60 day lag and revised for years. Context only. Backtests MUST use the
  first print, never the current value.

POWER_DEMAND_MU - all-India electricity demand  [BLOCKED - host gone]
  THE BEST HIGH-FREQUENCY ACTIVITY PROXY IN INDIA. Daily, barely revised,
  and available roughly six weeks before the IIP print it anticipates.
  Factories consume electricity before they report output.
  Needs weather adjustment: a hot April raises demand through air
  conditioning, not industry, and mistaking one for the other is the classic
  error with this series.

GST_COLLECTIONS / EWAY_BILLS  [PLANNED]
  GST prints on the 1st for the prior month - among the fastest real-activity
  reads available anywhere in India. E-way bills track goods physically
  moving and lead the GST number itself.

UPI_VOLUME / UPI_VALUE  [PLANNED]
  Consumption and formalisation. Structurally trending upward for years, so
  ONLY the deviation from trend carries information. The level is meaningless.

AUTO_REGISTRATIONS / TWO_WHEELER_REG / TRACTOR_REG  [PLANNED]
  Daily, granular, and hard to fake. Two-wheelers and tractors are the
  standard rural demand proxy and lead reported auto-sector earnings by weeks.
  Rural distress shows up here before it shows up in any official statistic.

BANK_CREDIT_YOY  [PLANNED]
  Credit growth is both a cause and a symptom. Accelerating credit signals
  investment appetite; decelerating credit signals either weak demand or
  banks refusing to lend, which are very different and worth distinguishing.


================================================================================
  4. EQUITY MARKET STATE  [LIVE]
================================================================================

NIFTY50_CLOSE / BANKNIFTY_CLOSE / NIFTY_MIDCAP_CLOSE
  Price is information. The large/mid dispersion matters enormously in India:
  in the IL&FS episode mid-caps fell roughly THREE TIMES as far as large
  caps. Treating "Indian equity" as one exposure misses this entirely.

INDIA_VIX - implied volatility
  The market's own forecast of near-term turbulence, from Nifty option
  prices. Spikes precede or accompany falls - it is the fear gauge. Very low
  VIX indicates complacency, which is itself a risk factor: positioning gets
  crowded when nobody expects trouble.

EQ_DELIV_PCT - delivery percentage
  What fraction of traded volume was actually delivered rather than
  intraday-squared-off. High delivery on a rise suggests genuine
  accumulation; low delivery suggests speculation that will unwind.

NIFTY_PE / NIFTY_PB  [PLANNED]
  Valuation. Poor at timing - expensive markets stay expensive for years -
  but the best available predictor of 5-to-10-year returns. India persistently
  trades at a premium to other emerging markets; whether that premium is
  deserved is the single biggest open question about this market.


================================================================================
  5. DERIVATIVES AND POSITIONING  [LIVE]
================================================================================

PCR_NIFTY - put-call ratio
  Open interest in puts divided by calls. High means heavy downside hedging
  or bearish positioning. Read as a CONTRARIAN signal at extremes: when
  everyone is hedged, the marginal seller is exhausted. Thresholds drift, so
  z-score it against a trailing window rather than using a fixed level.

NIFTY_IV_ATM - at-the-money implied volatility
  With the full option chain this yields the market's entire probability
  distribution for the index (see docs/VS_ALADDIN.md). It is the benchmark
  Vyuha scores its own forecasts against.

FII_INDEX_FUT_LS - foreign investor long-short ratio  [PLANNED]
  Direct positioning data, published daily. Extremes have historically
  preceded reversals better than any valuation measure.

MAX_PAIN
  The strike at which most options expire worthless. Weak evidence of price
  pinning near expiry; included for completeness, not conviction.


================================================================================
  6. FLOWS - WHO IS ACTUALLY BUYING  [LIVE]
================================================================================

FII_NET_CASH - foreign institutional flows
  The marginal price-setter in Indian large caps. Foreign investors trade on
  global risk appetite, the dollar and relative valuations, so this is the
  channel through which a Fed decision becomes an Indian equity move.
  Direction: sustained FII selling -> large caps DOWN and rupee DOWN
  simultaneously, because they sell the shares and then sell the rupees.

DII_NET_CASH - domestic institutional flows
  Increasingly the stabilising counterweight, funded by monthly SIP
  contributions that arrive regardless of market level. Watch DIVERGENCE:
  FII selling absorbed by DII buying is very different from both selling.

SIP_INFLOW  [PLANNED]
  Arguably the most important single number for Indian equities on a 1-3 year
  view. A structural, price-insensitive monthly bid. The bull case for India
  substantially rests on it continuing; a sharp fall would be a regime change.

FPI_NET_DEBT  [PLANNED]
  Foreign flows into bonds, driven by the India-US rate differential and
  index inclusion. Often moves opposite to equity flows.


================================================================================
  7. THE RUPEE AND EXTERNAL BALANCE
================================================================================

USDINR  [LIVE]
  Transmits in several directions at once, which is why it is hard:
    -> IT and pharma exporters EARN more rupees when it weakens
    -> importers and oil refiners PAY more
    -> foreign investors' returns fall when it weakens, encouraging outflows
    -> imported inflation rises, constraining the RBI
  FOR AN INDIAN INVESTOR HOLDING FOREIGN ASSETS this is not a side issue: a
  rupee investor's return is (1+asset return) x (1+rupee depreciation) - 1.
  Measured over the year to 2026-09-17, the S&P 500 returned +15.2% in
  dollars while the rupee weakened 8.9%, so an Indian holder received +25.4%
  - THE CURRENCY WAS 35% OF THE RETURN.

FX_RESERVES / CAD_PCT_GDP  [PLANNED]
  India's vulnerability gauge. A wide current account deficit with thin
  reserve cover is precisely the configuration that made India one of the
  "Fragile Five" in 2013, when the rupee fell from 55 to 68 in four months.


================================================================================
  8. COMMODITIES  [LIVE]
================================================================================

BRENT / WTI - crude oil
  INDIA'S SINGLE LARGEST EXTERNAL VULNERABILITY. Over 85% of crude is
  imported, so an oil spike hits four channels simultaneously:
    -> current account deficit widens -> rupee falls
    -> fuel and transport costs rise -> CPI rises -> RBI constrained
    -> the fuel subsidy bill rises -> fiscal deficit widens
    -> input costs rise across manufacturing -> margins compress
  Almost nothing else in this list transmits through four channels at once.
  Winners: upstream producers (ONGC). Losers: refiners, airlines, paints,
  tyres, anything with oil as a feedstock.

GOLD_USD_OZ / SILVER_USD_OZ - LBMA London fix
  Gold occupies a position in India it holds nowhere else: the largest private
  household holding in the world, a savings vehicle, and a major import. Gold
  imports widen the current account directly. Also a risk-off asset, so it
  tends to rise when equities fall.
  ALWAYS check the rupee price: gold can rise in dollars and fall in rupees.

COPPER / ALUMINIUM - industrial metals
  Global industrial demand proxies ("Dr Copper"). Monthly here, so context
  rather than signal.

WHEAT - grain prices
  Food inflation, and therefore the policy rate, and therefore equities.


================================================================================
  9. GLOBAL CONDITIONS  [LIVE]
================================================================================

US10Y / US2Y / FED_FUNDS
  The global risk-free rate. When US yields rise, capital leaves emerging
  markets for safer dollar assets. The 2013 taper tantrum is the archetype:
  a Fed signal, not an Indian event, took the rupee from 55 to 68.

DXY / DOLLAR_INDEX
  A strong dollar is systematically bad for emerging markets - it tightens
  global financial conditions, raises the cost of dollar debt, and pushes
  capital home.

SP500 / NASDAQ / VIX
  Global risk appetite. Indian equities are high-beta to it: correlations
  that sit near 0.3 in calm markets go above 0.9 in a crisis, which is
  exactly when diversification is supposed to help and does not.

GDP_GROWTH / INFLATION / MARKET_CAP_GDP (217 countries)
  Annual, heavily revised. Useful for comparing economies, useless for timing.


================================================================================
  10. WEATHER  [PLANNED] - the variable almost no risk system models
================================================================================

MONSOON_DEPARTURE_PCT / RESERVOIR_LEVEL
  One of the longest genuinely causal chains in this market, and it starts
  with rainfall:

    rainfall -> kharif crop output -> food prices -> CPI (food is 46% of the
    basket) -> RBI policy rate -> discount rate -> equity multiples

  In parallel: rainfall -> rural incomes -> two-wheeler, tractor and FMCG
  demand -> those companies' earnings.

  A failed monsoon is a genuine macro event in India. Very few global risk
  systems treat weather as a financial factor; here it is not optional.


================================================================================
  11. POLICY, GOVERNANCE AND SENTIMENT
================================================================================

SEBI / RBI CIRCULARS  [PLANNED]
  Unstructured text, high impact. A margin-requirement or position-limit
  change can reprice an entire market segment overnight. This is the category
  no statistical model can anticipate - demonetisation removed 86% of
  currency by value with no warning - and the main argument for keeping a
  policy-literate reader in the loop.

PROMOTER_PLEDGE_PCT  [PLANNED]
  Shares pledged by controlling shareholders as loan collateral. Rising
  pledging has preceded a large share of Indian mid-cap collapses: when the
  price falls, lenders sell the collateral, which pushes the price down
  further. A reflexive doom loop with no clean US analogue.

NEWS_TONE_INDIA  [LIVE]
  GDELT news tone. Noisy with a drifting baseline, so use the z-score against
  a trailing window, never the raw level. Extremes are contrarian.


================================================================================
  12. MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE - India-specific risks
================================================================================

CIRCUIT LIMITS
  A stock locked at its price band cannot be sold AT ANY PRICE. This is the
  failure mode that turns a modelled loss into an unmodelled one, and it is
  largely absent from risk systems designed for developed markets.

AMIHUD ILLIQUIDITY / DAYS-TO-LIQUIDATE
  Several thousand listed names, of which only a few hundred can absorb a
  large position within a day. The risk number that matters in a crisis is
  not "how far could this fall" but "how far could it fall before I can get
  out".

INDEX CONCENTRATION
  A handful of corporate groups carry meaningful Nifty weight, so "diversified
  across the Nifty" is less diversified than it sounds.

================================================================================
  THE LIMIT ON ALL OF THIS
================================================================================

  More variables does not mean more accuracy. Test 100 unrelated series
  against Nifty returns and about five clear p<0.05 on noise alone. Vyuha
  therefore requires out-of-sample survival with false-discovery-rate
  correction before trusting any new variable (docs/LEARNING.md).

  And no system resolves arbitrarily fine effects: NSE's tick size is 5
  paise, which on a Rs 500 share is a 0.01% quantum. Claims of finer
  resolution are false (docs/PRECISION.md).
